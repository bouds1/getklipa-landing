Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Klipa is operated by Boudreau Ventures LLC in Grand Rapids, Michigan. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Klipa landing page at getklipa.com, how we use it, and how you can contact us.

Information We Collect

If you join the Klipa waitlist, we collect your email address.

If you submit a partner inquiry, we may collect your name, store or chain name, role, email address, phone number if provided, and any message you choose to send.

How We Use Information

We use waitlist information to send Klipa launch updates, beta availability, and related product communications.

We use partner inquiry information to respond to retailer, grocer, or business partnership requests, including sending a partner one-pager or following up about possible collaboration.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We use Web3Forms to process form submissions from this website. When you submit a waitlist or partner form, the information you provide is processed by Web3Forms so it can be delivered to us.

We may also disclose information if required to comply with law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of Klipa, Boudreau Ventures LLC, our users, or others.

Cookies And Analytics

The current Klipa landing page does not use advertising pixels, behavioral tracking cookies, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar cookie-based analytics.

Data Retention

We keep waitlist and partner inquiry information for as long as needed to communicate with you about Klipa, manage launch interest, respond to business inquiries, and maintain reasonable business records.

You can ask us to remove your information by contacting us at jake@getklipa.com.

Children's Privacy

Klipa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through this website, but no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Klipa develops. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of your information, contact us at jake@getklipa.com.
